# Overview

Little Miracles' visual identity leans warm and reassuring rather than clinical or corporate. The palette reads as playful but trustworthy, striking a balance between "fun for kids" and "credible for parents". The colors give a gentle, organic softness without being childish or overbearing. Typography is rounded and friendly, reinforcing warmth over formality. Overall, the brand is trying to evoke safety, nurturing, and genuine care, while still signaling a "proven," structured educational approach, reassuring an anxious parent that this is a place that's both loving and competent.
