if a child’s needs cannot be safely met within the service, and reasonable adjustments have been explored but are not sufficient to ensure the safety, wellbeing, or meaningful inclusion of the child or others, the following steps will be considered:
- Review all strategies, supports, and adjustments to identify further options
- Engage collaboratively with the family, educators, and, where appropriate, external professionals to explore alternatives
- Consider referral to a more specialised service or support network better suited to meet the child’s individual needs
- Provide families with clear information, guidance, and support throughout the process
- This approach ensures that any decision regarding the continuation of care is thoughtful, transparent, and prioritises the safety, wellbeing, and inclusion of all children and educators within the Little Miracles community
