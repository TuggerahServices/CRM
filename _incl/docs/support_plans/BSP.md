BSP

1. NS organise a Family meeting with the Room Leader to discuss collaboration.
2. NS, RL, AM create BSP including strategies and commencement date (the next Monday). NS and RL 10 minute convo with family explaining plan and strategies. Followed by an email with the BSP, BSP Process package.
3. BSP In practice (Room has process to follow)
4. Weeks 1-4 NS spend 1hr a week in the room. Maybe a notes section for them weekly + Room Staff weekly notes.
5. Week 5 NS, AM, RL review strategies and their progress. If a child no longer displays challenging behaviours the NS will discuss with AM and cease BSP at any point from here on. If a child shows significant hard to children or educators they may be released from care immediately from any point here and on. Otherwise organise 2nd family meeting.
6. Week 6 - 2nd Family Meeting.
- Update and progress of BSP
7. Week 7-9
- Update plan accordingly rom previous weeks and family meeting
- NS and RL 10min conversation with the family if they wish followed by an email with the updated plan 
- NS spend 1hr a week in the room. Maybe a notes section for them weekly + Room Staff weekly notes.
- Week 9 NS and AM RL review 2nd strategies and its effectiveness.
8. Following consultation with the NS and RL/educators the AM will decide from the following options of the next action step:
Week 10
Option 1: Continued Care End of BSP
If care is sustainable and the child is no longer requiring ‘Extra Support’ the child’s ‘BSP’ will end.
Option 2: Release of care
If no significant change has been achieved, Nominated Supervisor is to notify the Area Manager that the child’s level of individual care continues to be unreasonable or a risk to others safety. The Area Manager will take steps to finalise/end child’s care.
